How a session runs

Thirty seconds to set up. Ten minutes that feel like the real thing.

  1. 01

    Pick your stream and role

    BSc IT, BCA, BCom, BBA, MBA and more. Add your resume or a real job description if you have one.

  2. 02

    Choose who interviews you

    A warm HR partner, a curious tech lead, a formal panel chair, a fast founder — or Stress mode.

  3. 03

    Answer, and get pushed

    Vague answers get a follow-up. Strong ones raise the difficulty. Nothing is scripted end-to-end.

  4. 04

    Read the debrief

    Scores, missed points, filler heatmap, timing, STAR breakdown, readiness and what to fix next.

Honest by design

Camera analysis runs in your browser on tiny thumbnails and only sends back numbers like “lighting: dim”. It never claims to read emotions, personality or honesty. Predictions are practice estimates, and they say so. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sessions use httpOnly cookies, and you can delete your account and every recording in one click.
